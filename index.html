01The houseSuzhou · since 2019

A Chinese house,
at home in

Huishuo is an ingredient and development house based in Suzhou, China. Everything the house ships leaves Chinese ports — and arrives the way formulators everywhere expect it to arrive.

The house supplies six industries, from personal care to industrial chemistry, and develops what the portfolio doesn't yet hold: formulations, finished product lines and tailored industrial projects, built in its own laboratory.

One way of working carries all of it — quiet, specified in writing, verified before it ships. That is the standard the name stands on.
